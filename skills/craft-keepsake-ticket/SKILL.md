# Craft Keepsake Ticket

Use this skill for A bright two-dimensional travel keepsake made from photography, paper, ink, and small acts of remembering work in `apps/keepsake-ticket`. Preserve rights and evidence boundaries; never publish or claim approval.

## Workflow

1. Choose a real travel or everyday photograph and keep its original framing intact.
2. Mark one meaningful point and apply a date stamp with immediate paper-and-ink feedback.
3. Tear the perforated stub to reveal the finished 3:4 photo-and-ticket keepsake.
4. Restart with the same or a different photograph without retaining private image data.
