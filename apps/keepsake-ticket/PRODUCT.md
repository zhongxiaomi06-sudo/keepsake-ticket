# Keepsake Ticket Product

## User and promise

Turn one real travel or everyday photo into a personal illustrated ticket through three tactile gestures.

## Core loop

`Start → Mark one memory point, stamp the date, then tear the ticket stub → Immediate themed feedback → Reveal a clean 3:4 original-photo and keepsake-ticket composition ready to keep → Restart or Done`

## Scope

Mark one memory point, stamp the date, then tear the ticket stub. Completion: Reveal a clean 3:4 original-photo and keepsake-ticket composition ready to keep.

## Current preview slice

The working surface, 3:4 photo-to-ticket composition, generated sample pair, local photo chooser, privacy note, and mute preference are implemented. Memory-point placement, date stamping, tearing, final export, and restart remain gated behind Owner visual approval.

## Explicit exclusions

3D scenes, models, rendering, or spatial navigation; AI resurrection or fabricated subjects not present in the photograph; Cloud persistence, accounts, social feed, print ordering, or blockchain collectibles; Brand marks, watermarks, promotional calls to action, or complex template selection

## Acceptance

Complete the core loop on the current formal iPhone/iOS and Web fallback.
