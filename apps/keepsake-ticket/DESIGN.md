# Keepsake Ticket Design

## Target visual direction

A bright two-dimensional travel keepsake made from photography, paper, ink, and small acts of remembering. Use a strict vertical 3:4 composition split evenly: unchanged photography above and a restrained cream-paper ticket below. Keep generous warm-white space, editorial serif type, low-saturation colors sampled from the photo, rounded ink stamps, a perforated stub, and light hand-painted illustration. Stay flat and tactile; use no 3D rendering, glossy gradients, dense collage, or commercial poster styling.

## Typography and copy

Concise en-US with readable system text and expressive display type only where thematic.

## Motion and sound

A quiet instrumental travel-diary bed with restrained paper tap, rubber stamp, perforation, and reveal cues. Unlock after the first valid interaction and persist mute.
